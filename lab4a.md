# Waffle Recipe

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

## Cooking Steps

1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
2. Add the eggs and milk.
3. Add the melted butter and mix well.
4. Pour the batter into the waffle maker.
5. Cook until the waffles are ready.


# Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to maintain a high standard of
**academic integrity** at Seneca and support teaching, learning,
research, and working practices.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Current students
- Seneca employees

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> Academic integrity means following the fundamental values of
> honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility, and courage.

### Cheating

**Cheating** is getting or trying to get academic credit through
dishonest or deceptive methods.

### Plagiarism

**Plagiarism** is using another person's or organization's work
without properly giving credit to the original source.

### Contract Cheating

**Contract cheating** happens when a student submits work created
by someone else as their own work.

### Unauthorized Aid

**Unauthorized aid** means using supports that are not approved
for an assignment or assessment.

## Policy

The main policy sections are:

- **1. General**
- **2. Academic Integrity Program**
- **3. Responsibilities**
- **4. Sanctions**

### 1. General

Seneca supports a learning community based on academic integrity,
honesty, fairness, trust, respect, responsibility, and courage.

Seneca also aims to provide high-quality education in a positive,
equitable, and inclusive learning environment.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

Academic integrity helps ensure that Seneca credentials accurately
represent the knowledge and learning demonstrated by students.

The Academic Integrity Program includes policies, procedures,
faculty development, training, and student programming.

### 3. Responsibilities

Seneca is responsible for providing students with information and
resources about academic integrity.

Students are responsible for informing faculty about circumstances
that may affect their academic performance.

Students may also report suspected academic integrity violations
using the procedures in Appendix A.

### 4. Sanctions

**Sanctions** depend on the seriousness of the academic integrity
violation.

Before a final decision is made, the student's record and relevant
circumstances may be considered.

Some cases may also involve other Seneca policies, such as the
Student Code of Conduct.

## Appendix A - Academic Integrity Procedure

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Report a suspected academic integrity violation | As soon as possible | Faculty, student, invigilator, or support staff |
| Inform the student and discuss the situation | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, or support staff |
| Collect the assessment and related evidence | Within five days | Faculty, invigilator, or support staff |
